\opening{Dear Editor-in-Chief,}

Please consider the manuscript ``From terawatt-scale semiconductor output to
grid burden: A prospective constraint assessment of the announced Terafab
project'' for publication in \emph{Energy Conversion and Management}.

The paper develops a dimensionally consistent link between semiconductor
product output, wafer throughput, facility electricity and heat rejection,
water withdrawal, supply portfolios and electricity-security screening. Its
central contribution is methodological: it evaluates an unusually large
public industrial claim without equating product rated power with factory
load, inventing private project capacities, or turning reserve margin into a
probabilistic reliability finding. The complete calculation is evidence-gated,
uncertainty-aware and reproducible from frozen public extracts.

Under the primary conditional interpretation, the full announced target uses
135.17 TWh of electricity per year and 16.58 GW coincident load in the central case. The
paper's scientific conclusion is deliberately bounded: current public evidence
does not demonstrate project realism, but this absence is not proof of
infeasibility. We believe this combination of energy-conversion scaling,
facility thermodynamics and regional/national energy-management consequences
fits the journal's scope.

All ten prespecified figures have machine-readable source tables; explicit
replacement figures disclose where independent validation, LOLE/EUE, national
peak, fuel or emissions data are unavailable. The manuscript contains a
generative-AI declaration, competing-interest disclosure and source-available
software notice. It does not claim Terafab affiliation, endorsement, verified
operating information, engineering certification or investment advice.

The author will confirm correspondence details, funding, exact software release
identifier and AI model/version in the submission system before upload. The
manuscript is original work and will be submitted only after the author has
completed the journal's authorship, exclusivity and permissions declarations.

Thank you for your consideration.
