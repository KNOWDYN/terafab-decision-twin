\section{Purpose and status}

This supplement documents the reproducible evidence and calculation package
for an independent prospective conditional constraint analysis of the announced
Terafab project. It contains no verified Terafab operating data and must not be
read as engineering certification, an official feasibility assessment, or a
prediction. The repository is source-available, not open source. Academic and
commercial rights are governed by the two root license files.

\section{Frozen evidence protocol}

The evidence registry records source identity, publisher, publication date,
URL, source class, evidence status, admissible use and limitation. Frozen JSON
extracts contain only admitted public values. Their hashes are recorded in
\texttt{evidence/snapshot\_manifest.json}; final reproduction does not access
the web. The source classes are:

\begin{enumerate}
\item official project public statement and applicant public filings;
\item manufacturer public disclosures and product specifications;
\item peer-reviewed measured cases or reviews;
\item government-laboratory benchmarks;
\item official ERCOT, PUCT, EIA and NERC projections, standards and studies.
\end{enumerate}

Public claims are scenario inputs, not verified facts. The study excludes
restricted, confidential, proprietary and unpublished project material. A
bibliographic audit during Step 6 corrected the Hu et al. DOI from the erroneous
registry suffix \texttt{ctz040} to the publisher-confirmed \texttt{ctz041}; no
parameter or numerical result changed.

\section{Scenario experiment}

The complete scenario matrix contains 337 trajectories: one no-build
counterfactual and 336 nonzero combinations of three target labels, four
realization pathways, four supply portfolios and seven stress conditions. Of
the nonzero trajectories, 224 research-fab or initial-large-scale cases are
non-numeric because public phase output capacities are unavailable. The 112
full-target trajectories are computed annually through 2050. The complete
matrix is exported as
\path{tables/table_04_scenario_matrix.csv}; annual and gate outputs are
included in the supplementary artifact manifest.

The target identities, facility energy and water balances, supply
commissioning, accredited capacity and tri-state gates are implemented in the
\texttt{terafab\_energy\_security} package. The notebook and command line call
the same functions and do not duplicate governing calculations.

\section{Uncertainty design}

The locked design has ten uncertain manufacturing and facility inputs, fixed
seed 20260801, scrambled Sobol sample sizes 16,384 and 32,768, a Gaussian copula
and $\pm0.20$ correlation stresses. Convergence is tested on median wafer
starts, median electricity, p95 facility peak and p95 water withdrawal. The
strict criterion is relative change below 0.01 for every metric and dependence
case. The observed maximum is 0.0028999879.

The independent Sobol and dependence-aware Shapley results diagnose
conditional facility peak. They do not answer H4's locked outcome of joint
feasibility or realization year, which is unidentifiable while indispensable
gates are unknown. Marginal p95 outputs must not be combined into a joint
design point.
